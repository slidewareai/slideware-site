Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Slideware LLC ("Slideware," "we," "us") handles information when you use our apps, our website at slideware.ai, or our services. Questions? Email support@slideware.ai.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. For example, your email address and message when you contact us, or account details if an app asks you to sign in.
  • Content you submit to AI features. Text, images, or other content you enter so an app can generate a result.
  • Usage and diagnostic data. Basic information such as app version, device type, crash reports, and how features are used, which helps us fix problems and improve our apps.
  • Purchase information. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by the app store or payment provider. We receive confirmation of the purchase, not your payment card details.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our apps and services.
  • To process requests you make with AI features.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate with you.
  • To detect, prevent, and fix abuse, security issues, and bugs.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run our apps, such as cloud hosting, analytics and crash reporting, and AI model providers that process content you submit to AI features. They may only use your information to provide services to us and must protect it.

How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing support@slideware.ai. If an app lets you create an account, you can also delete it from within the app. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and change the effective date above.
